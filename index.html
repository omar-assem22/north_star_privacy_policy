1. Information Collection and Use

The App uses device sensors (magnetometer/compass and accelerometer) locally on your device to display heading, direction, and orientation data. Sensor data is processed entirely in real-time on your hardware and is never collected, stored, or transmitted to any server by us.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for personalized or non-personalized advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.

AdMob may automatically collect data such as:

  • IP address (used to estimate general coarse location)
  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  • App performance and diagnostic logs
  • User interactions with ads

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

3. Data Handling & Security

All data transmitted by third-party SDKs (such as AdMob) is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We do not maintain any external databases or user accounts for NorthStar.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not collect or store personal user data on any developer-managed server, we do not retain any user records. You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime via your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
